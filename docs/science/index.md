# Scientific background

These pages explain what PROWL computes, how it computes it, and how to judge
whether a result can be trusted. They assume you have read the
[key concepts](../getting-started/concepts.md) or are already familiar with
crystal orbital analyses.

[Interpret the observables](interpreting-results.md)
: The definitions of COHP, COOP, COBI, pDOS and atomic charges, and what each
  tells you about bonding.

[Method and conventions](method.md)
: The projection, the reconstruction of the local Hamiltonian, band windows,
  sign and indexing conventions, and comparison with LOBSTER.

[Assess projection quality](quality.md)
: What each diagnostic in the quality report measures and a practical
  convergence procedure.

A local-orbital analysis describes a chosen representation of the electronic
structure, not a unique property of the material. Read the definitions together
with the conventions and the quality diagnostics.
